# Universal Tool Domain Glossary

This glossary defines the canonical domain terminology across the eight feature pillars of **Universal Tool**. It describes player-facing concepts and gameplay semantics only, without implementation details.

---

## 1. Universal Tool & Repair

- **Universal Tool (万能工具)**  
  A five-in-one multi-tool uniting the mining capabilities, combat attributes, and right-click world interactions (stripping logs, scraping copper/wax, tilling soil, flattening paths, extinguishing campfires) of a pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and sword. Available in six material tiers—Wood, Stone, Iron, Golden, Diamond, and Netherite—with 1.5× durability over standard tools.
- **Universal Repair Material (万能修复材料)**  
  A polymerized restoration catalyst consumed alongside experience levels in an anvil to restore any damageable item to full durability while preserving all enchantments, custom names, lore, and armor trims.

---

## 2. Astrolabe Polymerization

- **Universal Polymerizer (万能聚合台)**  
  An arcane-tech synthesis station arranged as a 12-node radial astrolabe surrounding a central polymerization core.
- **12-Ring Orderless Synthesis (十二环无序聚合)**  
  A crafting paradigm in which ingredients placed anywhere along the 12 outer ring slots are matched by multiset composition rather than fixed slot position, supporting tiered upgrades and high-tier item synthesis.

---

## 3. Spatial Waypoint & Codex

- **Teleport Codex (时空法典)**  
  A personal spatial astrolabe that binds waypoints across dimensions. Starts with 12 waypoint slots and can be expanded up to 36 slots.
- **Spatial Waypoint (时空坐标)**  
  A saved anchor capturing a target dimension, position, facing orientation, and an optional scenic snapshot.
- **Spatial Page (时空书页)**  
  An expansion parchment applied while holding a Teleport Codex in the off-hand to permanently increase the codex's waypoint capacity by 4 slots.
- **Teleport Scroll (时空传送卷轴)**  
  A portable, single-use scroll imprinted from a Teleport Codex waypoint. Can be channeled for a one-time warp or transcribed into another player's codex.
- **Scenic Snapshot (时空留影)**  
  A visual preview image captured when recording a waypoint inside the Teleport Codex interface.

---

## 4. Operation Phoenix

- **Operation Phoenix Pod (生化培养仓)**  
  A two-block-tall octagonal bio-incubation chamber that cultivates a dormant clone vessel and intercepts fatal harm to return the player alive.
- **DNA Syringe (DNA采集注射器)**  
  A medical extraction instrument used to draw a player's genetic material at the cost of a small amount of health.
- **DNA Sample (DNA样本)**  
  A genetic blueprint bound to a specific player, required to seed a clone inside an Operation Phoenix Pod.
- **Nutrient Solution (生物营养原液)**  
  An active biomass catalyst used to cultivate a clone. When activated while sneaking, it also encodes a full 41-slot combat loadout snapshot into the solution.
- **Quantum Consciousness Transfer (意识量子传输)**  
  The emergency rebirth sequence triggered upon lethal damage when a ready Phoenix Pod exists: fatal death is intercepted, consciousness warps back to the pod, the twin glass doors slide open, and any encoded loadout snapshot is reconstructed.

---

## 5. Pocket Realm

> **Disambiguation:** *Pocket Realm (口袋界域)* refers exclusively to a player's private 512×512 island dimension accessed through physical portal gates. It is distinct from *Klein Hyper-Storage* (portable item warehousing).

- **Pocket Realm (口袋界域)**  
  A private, persistent dimension owned by a single player, centered on a 512×512 sculptural floating island surrounded by navigable void. Falling into the void safely rescues the traveler back to the realm's spawn anchor.
- **Realm Gate (界域入口门)**  
  A crafted portal block bound to its placer. Multiple Realm Gates placed by the same owner lead to the same Pocket Realm; the owner enters freely, while visitors must authenticate with the realm's passphrase.
- **Return Gate (返回门)**  
  A protected, indestructible exit portal anchored within a Pocket Realm that returns travelers to their previous world location and dimension.
- **Realm Landscape (界域地貌)**  
  One of eight selectable environmental archetypes chosen when initializing a Pocket Realm:
  1. **Starlit Superflat (星辉超平坦)** — Level building expanse beneath open starlight.
  2. **Celestial Sky Archipelago (天穹浮空群岛)** — Clustered floating sky islands linked above the void.
  3. **Mystic Cherry Sanctuary (灵樱秘境)** — Blossom-crowned highlands with giant mystic cherry landmarks.
  4. **Amethyst Starfall Peaks (紫晶星陨峰)** — Calcite ridges studded with crystalline amethyst spires.
  5. **Sculk Echo Sanctum (幽匿回响圣殿)** — Deep-slate sanctuary lit by sculk veins and soul-lantern obelisks.
  6. **Bioluminescent Mushroom Wonderland (荧光蕈境奇观)** — Mycelial terrain overgrown with giant glowing mushrooms.
  7. **Gilded Obsidian Ring (鎏金黑曜环域)** — Volcanic blackstone and gilded obsidian basin with magma pillars.
  8. **Aurora Prism Glacier (极光棱镜冰川)** — Glacial expanses crowned with refractive prism ice spikes.

## 6. Skill Circuit

- **Skill Circuit (技能回路)**  
  An item-independent, persistent player progression system combining independent growth experience, unlockable active abilities, always-learnable passives, and hot-swappable loadout slots.
- **Growth Level & Skill Points (成长等级与技能点)**  
  A dedicated 50-level progression track earned through combat, mining, harvesting, and first-time world discoveries, granting 68 total skill points (including 5-level milestone bonuses)—enough to master every skill.
- **Active Skills (主动技能)**  
  Eight castable abilities—*Counter Guard*, *Gravity Pull*, *Frozen Pull*, *Oppressive Field*, *Ore Sight*, *Air Step*, *Lightning Spear*, and *Berserk*—each gated by an in-game unlock condition and governed by wall-clock cooldowns that continue recovering offline.
- **Passive Skills (被动技能)**  
  Twelve continuous traits—*Battle Momentum*, *Focused Mining*, *Last Stand*, *Survival Flow*, *Swift Pace*, *Light Feather*, *Iron Physique*, *Armor Piercing*, *Bloodthirst*, *Item Magnet*, *Diamond Bulwark*, and *Damage Clamp*—active whenever equipped in passive loadout slots.
- **Combat Lockout (战斗锁定)**  
  A 10-second post-combat safety window during which learning, upgrading, equipping, and resetting skills are restricted.

---

## 7. Klein Hyper-Storage

> **Disambiguation:** *Klein Hyper-Storage (克莱因超维仓储)* refers exclusively to the portable, non-Euclidean item warehousing system accessed via the Klein Bottle. It is distinct from the *Pocket Realm* island dimension.

- **Klein Bottle (克莱因瓶)**  
  A portable topological vessel that serves as the player's entry point to Klein Hyper-Storage, accessible via hotkey or item use.
- **Hyper-Storage Terminal (超维仓储终端)**  
  The unified warehousing interface offering arbitrary-precision item stack counts, live search and sorting, quick deposit, and integrated *Crafting Table*, *Hyper Smelting*, and *Anvil* workbenches.
- **Container Sweep (容器批量转入)**  
  An interaction triggered by sneak-right-clicking a chest-like container while holding a Klein Bottle to bulk-transfer storable contents into hyper-storage.
- **Auto-Deposit (自动入库)**  
  An optional per-player preference (disabled by default) that automatically routes newly picked-up items into Klein Hyper-Storage if that item type is already stocked.

---

## 8. Void Lottery

- **Universal Lottery Machine (万能抽奖机)**  
  A two-block arcane wagering device with three interactive modes—*Void Dice*, *Cosmic Roulette*, and *Void Slots 777*—that transforms sacrificed items into randomized tiered rewards.
- **Appraisal Value & Luck Bonus (献祭估值与幸运加成)**  
  A dynamic point valuation assigned to sacrificed items, further amplified by nearby Enchanting Tables, shifting probability weights toward higher rarity tiers.
- **Rarity Tiers & Jackpot (稀有度阶梯与十倍大奖)**  
  Six escalating reward tiers—*Common*, *Uncommon*, *Rare*, *Epic*, *Legendary*, and *Mythic*—plus a high-roll *Jackpot x10* multiplier.
- **Void Scrap (虚空残片)**  
  Inert dimensional residue yielded when a draw resolves into scrap; cannot be re-entered into the lottery machine.
